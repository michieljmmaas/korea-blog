## Heading to the Airport
I woke up on time and did some more preparations before checking out and flying back to Seoul. I booked a flight for 12:25, so that I would have some time left when I got back to Seoul. I took the monorail this time, which was a direct connection to the airport. I managed to empty my transit card till there was nothing left, and paid for the remaining tickets using my last bit of cash, while still keeping enough coins for souvenirs. 


I got through the security without issue, and had some time left to kill while waiting for my flight. I had some breakfast and worked on my blog before I was called to board the plane.

## The flight back
The flight itself went fine. Right before the plane was about to take off, the baby started crying, so I just increased the volume on my headphones. I worked on my blog some more, was served another meal, and landed about 2 hours after boarding.

I got through customs showing my K-ETA form, which is valid for two more years, and was back on the metro to my hotel. Even though I was gone for only a few days, it felt nice being back in Seoul. I could read, not fully understand, all the signage again. I stocked up on some more kimbaps before heading to my hotel room. 

I had planned to do some more administrative tasks, like update my finances, but it was 17:00 when I finally put down my bags and took off my shoes. 

<Img 6 desc="Back 'home'">

I was able to get some work done, but not everything I had planned to do. When I looked back up on the clock, it was already 20:00, and I was hungry. Too tired to go to a restaurant, I decided to eat some leftovers for dinner and call it a night. 

The trip back took more out of me than I was expecting, so there is still a lot left for me to do. Let's lock in these next couple of days so I can make the best of my last three weeks here!
